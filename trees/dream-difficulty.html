Difficulty

August 27, 2026

Today was difficult. I did a lot of physical labor (helping my friend move), I had a lot of hard conversations (while coaching), and then forced myself to climb on a rope after all of it.

But, it wasn't a bad day.

I didn't navigate every situation perfectly (accidentally took my friends parking permit for their new place), but I feel like I'm growing more than I ever have.

I feel gratitude for the things I get to do, joy for the successes of the day, pain for the people I feel like I should be able to help, anxiety for how the difficulties will resolve themselves, and hope for the future, all at once.

If I keep pushing myself to do these difficult things, it is inevitable that I'll fail at some point. Am I okay with that?

But, if I never push myself to do these things, I'll never grow. I'm not okay with that.
