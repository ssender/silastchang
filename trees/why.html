Why Does This Website Exist?

April 26, 2026

I like the idea of doing creative and/or stupid stuff and putting it on the internet, but I don't like social media.

I want to create things without the pressure of catering to an algorithm, and I want people who consume the content I make to be people who actively are looking for my stuff, not people that an algorithm decided would like my stuff.

I've also always liked the idea of making a video game, but making an entire game at once is too much of a commitment, so this website lets me release scenes/prototypes in tiny chunks.

Summary: need a way to scream into the void and put my creativity on the internet, but don't want to run a social media account because social media is evil and bad
